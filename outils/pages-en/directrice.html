Two generations, one shared mission

The eldest daughter of Simone Zoundi, the company’s founder, Yolande Alexise Zoundi now runs SODEPAL SA. She shares the same conviction: turning Burkina Faso’s cereals and pulses — maize, millet, soya and groundnuts — into foods that protect children’s health.

“This extension will enable us to increase our production.”Yolande Alexise Zoundi — inauguration, 6 March 2026

On 6 March 2026 she inaugurated the extension to the production unit, built with the World Food Programme: up to 36 tonnes of infant flours a day.

A word from the Managing Director

“Feeding our children better with what our land produces: that has been the purpose of our work since 1991.”

Dear visitors, for more than thirty years SODEPAL has been turning Burkina Faso’s maize, millet, soya and moringa into nutritious foods. Our fortified flours support children through weaning and help to prevent nutritional deficiencies.

True to our founder’s vision, in March 2026 we inaugurated the extension to our production unit, with support from the World Food Programme. Behind these 36 tonnes a day are empowered women, young people in work and better-fed families.

Families, retailers, health partners: we are here to listen.
