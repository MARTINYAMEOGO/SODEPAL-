6 March 2026 · Production

A bigger factory to feed children better

On Friday 6 March 2026, SODEPAL-SA inaugurated the extension to its infant flour production unit in Ouagadougou. The ceremony was held under the patronage of the Prime Minister, represented by the Minister Delegate for Animal Resources, Dr Amadou Dicko.

Built with support from the World Food Programme, which invested nearly 400 million CFA francs in construction, equipment and staff training, the unit cost more than 605 million CFA francs in total.

2026 · Quality

Towards ISO 22000 certification

Having adopted HACCP principles and obtained NBF certification for its flours, SODEPAL has begun the process of obtaining ISO 22000 certification, the international standard for food safety management. One more step towards guaranteeing the safety of products intended for children.

Our founder in the spotlight

The daily newspaper Sidwaya has published a profile of Simone Zoundi, founder of SODEPAL, describing her as “an icon of local food processing”. The article traces more than fifty years of commitment, from her first millet biscuits to the founding of SODEPAL in 1991.

28 November – 5 December 2025 · Event

The 13th Agri-Food Days in Ouagadougou

The 13th edition of the Agri-Food Days (JAAL) took place in Ouagadougou on the theme “Agri-food processing and food sovereignty in Burkina Faso: issues, challenges and prospects”. Initiated by Simone Zoundi, the event promotes locally processed products.
