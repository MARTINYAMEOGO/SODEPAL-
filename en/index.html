Who are we?

Making the most of what Burkina’s land produces

Founded on 23 December 1991 by Simone Zoundi, a pioneer of Burkina Faso’s food industry, SODEPAL SA is a manufacturing SME with share capital of 100 million CFA francs. It produces complementary nutritional foods from local produce, sold in Burkina Faso and across the sub-region.

Our goal: to fight chronic malnutrition, for the well-being of all.

  • Make the most of local resources and create jobs
  • Provide nutritious food that supports food security
  • Test every batch in our laboratory

Our story

A family business since 1991

It all began in the 1970s at the Gounghin bakery, with Simone Zoundi’s first millet biscuits. Two generations later, the same belief still drives SODEPAL: feeding children well with what Burkina’s land produces.

  1. 1973

    First millet biscuits at the Gounghin bakery.

  2. 1991

    On 23 December, Simone Zoundi founds SODEPAL in Ouagadougou.

  3. 2026

    Led by Yolande Alexise Zoundi, the factory now makes up to 36 t of flour a day.
