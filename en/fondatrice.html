Simone Zoundi, the founder

A pioneer of the food industry in Burkina Faso, she has devoted more than fifty years to processing local produce to fight child malnutrition.

A PIONEER

Simone Zoundinée Kafando · founder of SODEPAL SA

Portrait

A life devoted to local food processing

A 1968 graduate of the École supérieure de commerce et d’administration des entreprises (ESCAE), Simone Zoundi née Kafando was one of the first women to start a business in Burkina Faso. From the 1970s she was producing biscuits made from local cereals, before founding SODEPAL on 23 December 1991.

“I cannot bear to see children going hungry and malnourished.”Simone Zoundi — Sidwaya, 2025

A builder of businesses and professional organisations, she founded the National Federation of Agri-food and Processing Industries of Burkina (FIAB) and championed the Agri-food Days (JAAL). Today her daughter Yolande Alexise Zoundi runs SODEPAL as Managing Director.

Career

Five decades of commitment

  1. 1968

    Graduation and return home

    After graduating in business studies and management (ESCAE), she joined the Chamber of Commerce as deputy to the Director General.

  2. 1973 – 1974

    First steps in the food industry

    She took charge of the Gounghin confectionery and then its bakery, where she launched her first millet-based biscuits.

  3. 1976

    Professional Group of Industrialists

    Founding member of the Groupement professionnel des industriels (GPI).

  4. 1980

    Founding SIBB

    She founded the Société industrielle de biscuits et bonbons (SIBB) in Kossodo to fight child malnutrition. The company was nationalised in 1983.

  5. 1990

    Women entrepreneurs’ networks

    Founding member of the Association of Women Business Leaders and founder of the African Union of Women Managers.

  6. 1991

    The birth of SODEPAL and FIAB

    On 23 December 1991 she set up the Société d’exploitation des produits alimentaires (SODEPAL). The same year she founded FIAB, which today brings together more than 150 companies and associations.

  7. 1993 – 1996

    Economic and Social Council

    First Vice-President and Chair of the Economic and Legal Section of the Economic and Social Council.

  8. 2009 – 2010

    The Agri-food Days

    President of the Agri-food Days Association (AJAAL), which launched the JAAL to promote locally processed products.

  9. 2026

    A growing company

    On 6 March 2026 SODEPAL inaugurated the extension of its production unit, with support from the World Food Programme: up to 36 tonnes of infant flours a day.
