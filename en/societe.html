Who are we?

A Burkinabè SME dedicated to nutrition

Founded on 23 December 1991 by Simone Zoundi (née Kafando), a pioneer of Burkina Faso’s food industry, SODEPAL SA is a small manufacturing company with share capital of 100 million CFA francs. It produces nutritional supplement foods made from local resources, with a varied range sold on the national and regional markets.

Burkina Faso is an agricultural country with abundant crop and livestock resources, yet industrial processing remains a weak link in its economy. Chronic malnutrition is a public health problem that affects the health of children and mothers and causes economic and social losses.

SODEPAL exists to meet both of these challenges.

“Feeding our children better with what our land produces: that has been the purpose of our work since 1991.”

The founder’s eldest daughter, Yolande Alexise Zoundi, now runs SODEPAL. On 6 March 2026, she inaugurated the factory extension with the World Food Programme: up to 36 tonnes of infant flour a day.

Our journey

More than thirty years of expertise

From infant flours to wedding cakes, one ambition: to make the most of what Burkina Faso produces.

  1. 1991

    SODEPAL SA is founded

    On 23 December 1991, an SME with share capital of 100 million CFA francs is created, dedicated to nutritional supplement foods.

  2. Nutrition

    Fortified flours

    Vitaline (maize) and Vita Casui (millet), fortified with protein, vitamins and minerals, then NBF-certified.

  3. Expertise

    A wider range

    Pastries, biscuits and sweets, followed by Le Bessé, our honey and millet liqueur.

  4. 2026

    A larger plant

    On 6 March 2026, the extension of the production unit was inaugurated with WFP support: up to 36 tonnes of infant flour a day.

What guides us

Mission, goal and values

Our mission

Adding value to local resources

To add value to Burkina Faso’s agricultural, forestry and livestock resources, create jobs and help generate wealth by producing foods, nutritional supplements, pastries, biscuits and sweets.

Our goal

Fighting malnutrition

To combat chronic malnutrition for people’s well-being by supplying a variety of nutritious foods: real support for food and nutrition security.

Our values

What we stand for

  • Quality: HACCP rules and national and international standards
  • Local roots: raw materials from Burkina Faso
  • Nutrition: products that support good health
  • Employment: creating value locally

Our stakeholders

From field to plate

SODEPAL is one link in a chain that connects Burkina Faso’s producers with the families who eat our products.

  1. 01 · Producers

    Local producers

    They grow and harvest the maize, millet, moringa, sesame and honey that we process.

  2. 02 · SODEPAL

    Our teams

    Processing, fortification, laboratory testing and packaging, in Ouagadougou.

  3. 03 · Distribution

    Retailers & distributors

    They make our products available on the national and regional markets.

  4. 04 · Consumers

    Families & health partners

    Children of weaning age, people with nutritional deficiencies and the organisations that support them.
