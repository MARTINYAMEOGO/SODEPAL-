Frequently asked questions

Do you have a question?

Answers to the questions we are asked most often. For anything else, give us a call.

+226 25 36 10 82
Where is SODEPAL located?

Our premises are in Zogona, Ouagadougou, Rue 13.24, Porte 570. Get directions.

What sizes do your flours come in?

Vitaline and Vita Casui are available in 50 g, 100 g, 400 g, 500 g and 1 kg.

Are your flours certified?

Yes, our fortified flours are NBF-certified. All our production follows HACCP principles and is tested in our own laboratory.

How should the flours be stored?

Store in a dry, well-ventilated place.

How do I order a cake for an event?

Contact us by phone or through the form, choosing “Pastry order”: tell us the date, the number of guests and your ideas.

Can I become a reseller of your products?

Yes. Our products are distributed on the national and sub-regional markets. Write to us, choosing “Become a reseller”.

Do you offer internships?

You can send us an internship request or a speculative application using the form above.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday from 6:30 am to 6 pm, Sundays from 6:30 am to 2 pm.
