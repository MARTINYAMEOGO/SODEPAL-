Our laboratory

Testing for peace of mind

Our in-house laboratory ensures the quality of our products. Because our flours are intended for children of weaning age and people with nutritional deficiencies, we leave nothing to chance.

  • Compliance with HACCP system rules
  • Regular checks on how standards are applied
  • NBF-certified fortified flours
  • Ongoing staff awareness training
  • ISO 22000 certification process under way

Our commitments

Quality, checked at every stage

Four pillars that shape the work of all our teams.

  1. 01

    HACCP system

    Compliance with HACCP rules — hazard analysis and control of critical points — to assure the quality of all our products.

  2. 02

    National & international standards

    Regular checks on how standards are applied. Our fortified flours are NBF-certified.

  3. 03

    In-house laboratory

    Tests carried out on site to guarantee safe, compliant products.

  4. 04

    Quality-aware staff

    A monthly meeting between each department and the RAQ-Nutrition (quality assurance manager) keeps awareness constantly high.

From raw material to packet

Production under control

  1. 01

    Local ingredients

    Maize, millet, moringa, sesame, honey: resources from Burkina Faso.

  2. 02

    HACCP production

    Processing and fortification in line with hygiene and safety rules.

  3. 03

    Lab testing

    Our products are analysed in our in-house laboratory.

  4. 04

    Packaging

    Packets from 50 g to 1 kg, to be stored in a dry, well-ventilated place.
